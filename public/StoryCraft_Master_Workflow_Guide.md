## 1. Character Consistency Formula (The Identity Lock)
To guarantee 0% face morphing across video cuts, always append these exact anchor tokens to your positive prompt:

`definitive consistent character design, retain identical facial features, same face, consistent facial structure, fixed hairstyle, identical face morphology`

### Negative Constraints (Video Anti-Warping):
`deformed, blurry, ugly, distorted face, extra limbs, modern objects, inconsistent character, low resolution, watermark, floating eyes, shifting hair texture`
